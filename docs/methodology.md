# Methodology

## 1. Eigenmode validation

Two rectangular vacuum cavities were created in CST Studio Suite. The first five modes were solved using the eigenmode solver and compared with the analytical rectangular-cavity equation.

## 2. Practical excitation

The model was transferred to the frequency-domain solver. Coaxial probes with aluminium conductors and Teflon dielectric were added. Probe length, spacing and feed orientation were varied to find a stable coupling configuration.

## 3. Split-cavity study

The cavity was separated along the X direction using gaps equal to 5%, 10%, 15% and 20% of the total X dimension. Resonance frequency, bandwidth, electric-field distribution and loaded quality factor were compared with the unsplit cavity.

## 4. Material perturbation

A dielectric sample was inserted into the gap. Sample thickness and relative permittivity were varied while monitoring resonance frequency, bandwidth, field distribution and mode separation.

## 5. Post-processing

S-parameter data were exported from CST. The -3 dB bandwidth was determined from the frequency interval around the S21 transmission peak, and quality factor was calculated from resonance frequency divided by bandwidth.
