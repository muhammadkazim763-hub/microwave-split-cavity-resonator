# Thesis Data Summary

## Cavity dimensions

| Resonator | X | Y | Z | Volume |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| R1 | 140 mm | 30 mm | 80 mm | 336,000 mm3 |
| R2 | 695 mm | 100 mm | 550 mm | 38,225,000 mm3 |

## Probe studies

- R1 probe lengths: 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 mm
- R2 probe lengths: 20, 25, 30, 35 and 40 mm
- Connector-spacing range: 30%-80% of the relevant dimension
- Selected spacing: 70%
- Selected representative pin lengths: 7.5 mm for R1 and 38 mm for R2

## Gap study

- Evaluated gaps: 0%, 5%, 10%, 15% and 20% of cavity X dimension
- R1 remained acceptable up to approximately 5% gap in the reported Q-factor comparison
- A 10% gap was used for the dielectric-sample investigation
- The 20% gap did not retain useful resonant behaviour in the investigated configuration

## Material study

- Relative permittivity was varied above that of vacuum
- At 10% sample thickness, the estimated upper ranges were approximately 2.45 for R1 and 3.85 for R2
- Increasing permittivity lowered resonance frequency
- X-feed and Z-feed showed similar trends; Z-feed was selected for later analysis because it showed slightly higher electric-field intensity
