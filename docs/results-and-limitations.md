# Results and Limitations

## Main findings

- Analytical and CST eigenmode frequencies agreed closely for both cavities.
- Larger cavity dimensions shifted resonance to lower frequencies.
- Increasing probe spacing reduced mutual probe interaction and moved the practical resonance closer to the eigenmode result.
- Splitting the cavity reduced field confinement and quality factor.
- A dielectric sample reduced resonance frequency as relative permittivity increased.
- Resonator 2 produced a higher Q but required a much larger physical volume.

## Limitations

- The original CST project files and raw exported S-parameter datasets were not supplied for this public repository.
- The published figures and numeric tables are taken from the author's thesis document.
- The available thesis supports a simulation study; it does not document a fabricated resonator or VNA-based experimental validation.
- Some plotted trends cannot be reproduced point-by-point because the underlying CST export files are unavailable.
- The thesis PDF is not included because its conclusion chapter contains unfinished placeholder text.

The Python analysis reproduces the analytical frequency checks and Q-factor comparisons from the reported data. It does not recreate the CST electromagnetic solution.
